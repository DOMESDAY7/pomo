A free Pomofocus alternative

Pomofocus is a great Pomodoro timer, and its interface inspired this one. But several features are reserved for its paid plan. This timer offers them for free, with no account, and open source, and adds tools Pomofocus does not have in its free version.

When to choose Pomofocus

If you want your tasks and history on several devices with an account, Pomofocus is the better choice: here, everything stays in one browser (with CSV export for statistics).

Switching from Pomofocus

  1. Open the timer: same default durations (25, 5 and 15 minutes), same colors.
  2. Recreate your tasks, or import them from Todoist with your personal token (tasks ⋮ menu).
  3. Add a Discord webhook in the settings to notify your server when each session ends.
  4. Install it as an app to use it offline.
