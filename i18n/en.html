Free Online Pomodoro Timer

A simple Pomodoro timer with no ads, no account, and open source. Set your work and break durations, organize your tasks, track your statistics and get a browser notification or a message on Discord, Slack, ntfy or Gotify at the end of each session. It installs as an app and works offline.

What is the Pomodoro Technique?

The Pomodoro Technique is a time management method created by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s. It is named after the tomato-shaped kitchen timer he used. The idea: split your work into short, focused sessions separated by real breaks.

How to use this Pomodoro timer

  1. Add the task you are working on.
  2. Start the timer: 25 minutes of uninterrupted work.
  3. When it rings, take a 5-minute short break.
  4. Repeat. After 4 pomodoros, take a 15-minute long break.

Every duration can be changed in the Settings menu. Press Space to start and ? to see all shortcuts.

Features

  • Task list: estimated and finished pomodoros, estimated finish time, notes, reusable templates and Todoist import.
  • Floating mini timer: a small window that stays on top of your other apps (Chrome, Edge).
  • OBS overlay for study-with-me streams: transparent background, timer only, durations in the URL.
  • Statistics: pomodoros today and over the last 7 days, streak, top tasks, CSV export.
  • Sounds: four alarms, ticking, white, pink or brown noise to help you focus.
  • Browser notifications and webhooks for Discord, Slack, Mattermost, ntfy, Gotify or any custom service.
  • Keyboard shortcuts, dark mode, custom durations and auto-start.
  • Offline: installable as an app (PWA).
  • Privacy: everything stays in your browser. Lightweight, written in Rust and WebAssembly.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get a Discord notification when a pomodoro ends?

In Discord, open the channel settings, then Integrations, then Webhooks, create a webhook and copy its URL. In the timer settings, add a Discord webhook and paste the URL. The “Send a test” button checks that everything works.

How do I show the timer in OBS?

In the settings, under “OBS overlay”, copy the suggested URL and add it as a “Browser source” in OBS. The background is transparent and only the durations you picked are used.

Does the timer work offline?

Yes. The site installs as an app (PWA) and keeps working without a connection. When offline, webhooks are skipped and only the browser notification is shown.

Are my settings, tasks and statistics sent to a server?

No. Everything is stored in your browser. There is no account and no ads. Messages go straight from your browser to the service you picked.

Does the timer keep running if I switch tabs?

Yes. Time is computed from the planned end time, so it stays accurate even when the tab is in the background. The remaining time is also shown in the tab title.
