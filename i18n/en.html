Free Online Pomodoro Timer

A simple Pomodoro timer with no ads, no account, and open source. Set your work and break durations, get a browser notification at the end of each session and, if you want, a message on Discord, Slack, ntfy or Gotify. It installs as an app and works offline.

What is the Pomodoro Technique?

The Pomodoro Technique is a time management method created by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s. It is named after the tomato-shaped kitchen timer he used. The idea: split your work into short, focused sessions separated by real breaks.

How to use this Pomodoro timer

  1. Pick a task to work on.
  2. Start the timer: 25 minutes of uninterrupted work.
  3. When it rings, take a 5-minute short break.
  4. Repeat. After 4 pomodoros, take a 15-minute long break.

Every duration can be changed in the Settings menu.

Features

  • Custom durations: pomodoro, short break, long break and how often long breaks happen.
  • Task list: estimated and finished pomodoros per task, estimated finish time, reusable templates and Todoist import. All free.
  • Auto-start breaks and pomodoros, if you want.
  • Browser notifications and a sound at the end of each session.
  • Webhooks: Discord, Slack, Mattermost, ntfy, Gotify or any service with a custom request body. Several webhooks at once.
  • Offline: installable as an app (PWA), usable without a connection.
  • Privacy: settings are only stored in your browser.
  • Light and fast: written in Rust and compiled to WebAssembly.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get a Discord notification when a pomodoro ends?

In Discord, open the channel settings, then Integrations, then Webhooks, create a webhook and copy its URL. In the timer settings, add a Discord webhook and paste the URL. The “Send a test” button checks that everything works.

Does the timer work offline?

Yes. The site installs as an app (PWA) and keeps working without a connection. When offline, webhooks are skipped and only the browser notification is shown.

Are my settings and webhook URLs sent to a server?

No. Everything is stored in your browser. There is no account, no tracking and no ads. Messages go straight from your browser to the service you picked.

Does the timer keep running if I switch tabs?

Yes. Time is computed from the planned end time, so it stays accurate even when the tab is in the background. The remaining time is also shown in the tab title.
